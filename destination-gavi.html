Pathanamthitta Forests

Gavi

A regulated eco-tourism landscape of rainforest, reservoirs, cardamom country, birdlife and guided outdoor experiences.

Why Gavi

A quieter forest journey with conservation at its centre.

Gavi lies within the forested highlands of Pathanamthitta, close to the Periyar landscape. Kerala Forest Development Corporation programmes combine guided trekking, birdwatching, boating and forest stays while involving local communities in eco-tourism.

Forest Experiences

What To Experience In Gavi

Water

Gavi & Kochupamba Lakes

Choose an authorised boating experience for calm reservoir views framed by forest and grassland.

Guided Nature

Forest Trails & Birding

Join a supervised programme suited to your fitness. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, and forest instructions must be followed.

Scenic Approach

Plantations & Reservoirs

The approach through Vandiperiyar passes cardamom country, forest roads and reservoir scenery; allow extra time for controlled access.

Responsible Access

Plan around the forest, not against it.

Advance booking is important because entry passes and guided programmes are controlled. Weather, forest conditions and conservation restrictions can change access, so confirm directly before setting out and never approach or feed wildlife.

Official Gavi eco-tourism guide
Prepare Well

Keep the forest day simple.

  • Confirm the programme, permit and meeting point in advance
  • Wear suitable walking shoes and carry rain protection
  • Carry permitted water and take all waste back out
  • Confirm included meals and dietary requirements before arrival
Build It In

Use Gavi as a deliberate forest extension.

Gavi works best as a pre-booked one- or two-night add-on from Thekkady or Vandiperiyar. It is not included in the six published itineraries, so allow a separate forest day instead of squeezing it between major transfers.
