The Route

A demanding north-to-south Kerala journey.

Kochi remains the arrival gateway, followed by a long transfer to Wayanad. The route then crosses to Munnar and continues through Thekkady, two nights in Alappuzha, Varkala and Thiruvananthapuram. The two longest road days are clearly protected from extra sightseeing.

Day By Day

Your 11-Day Kerala Plan

Day01

Kochi Arrival + Wayanad Transfer

Arrive in Kochi and begin the approximately 270 km / 6.5-hour planning transfer to Wayanad. An early arrival is strongly recommended. Check in, have dinner and rest without adding major sightseeing.

  • Kochi arrival gateway
  • Long road transfer
  • Wayanad check-in and rest

Road note: Traffic, weather, ghat conditions and the exact Wayanad stay can extend this journey.

Day02

Wayanad: One Strong Local Circuit

Choose one compact circuit: coffee country around Meppadi, Lakkidi View Point and either Edakkal Caves, a weather-appropriate waterfall or another accessible nature stop.

  • Coffee-estate landscape
  • One viewpoint
  • One heritage or nature activity

Optional / choose based on interest: Chembra trekking area, Thirunelli Temple, Wayanad Heritage Museum, Soochipara, Meenmutty or Kuruva Island. Confirm weather, seasonal access, opening rules and physical difficulty.

Day03

Wayanad To Munnar: Long Transfer

Leave early for the approximately 360 km / 9-hour planning transfer. Build in a proper meal stop and at least two comfort breaks, then check in, dine and rest.

  • Early departure
  • Planned meal and comfort stops
  • No major sightseeing after arrival

Road note: This is the itinerary's longest transfer and may take longer with traffic, weather or roadworks.

Day04

Munnar: Tea Country

Use one realistic circuit around Eravikulam National Park when open, tea-estate scenery and two nearby stops such as Mattupetty, Echo Point, Kundala or a waterfall.

  • Eravikulam when available
  • Tea-estate scenery
  • Lake, viewpoint or waterfall stops

Optional / if time permits: Kolukkumalai sunrise, another waterfall, kayaking where available or an evening Kalaripayattu programme. Do not combine every option in one day.

Day05

Munnar To Thekkady

Allow approximately 106 km / 4 hours using the official conservative planning guidance. After check-in, choose one light spice plantation or Periyar-area activity.

  • Plantation-road transfer
  • Spice-country introduction
  • Light evening
Day06

Thekkady: Periyar + Spice Country

Choose a pre-booked Periyar Lake boat safari or another authorised nature experience. Add the spice visit if it was not completed on Day 5, then keep the evening for an optional cultural programme.

  • Authorised nature experience
  • Spice plantation
  • Optional Kathakali or Kalaripayattu

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed; follow reserve and operator guidance.

Day07

Thekkady To Alappuzha

Use the official conservative allowance of approximately 164 km / 5 hours. After arrival, check in and choose a light Alappuzha Beach or lighthouse evening.

  • Hill-to-backwater transfer
  • Alappuzha check-in
  • Light beach or lighthouse option
Day08

Alappuzha: Classic Backwaters

Choose either a longer daytime houseboat experience or a shorter shikara ride. Exact cruise duration, meals and refreshments depend on the operator and must be verified.

  • Option A: daytime houseboat experience
  • Option B: shorter shikara ride
  • One beach or heritage addition

Choose one: Alappuzha Beach for the classic town coast or Marari for a quieter beach. Fit Alappuzha Lighthouse or Ambalappuzha Sree Krishna Temple around the boating plan only when practical.

Day09

Alappuzha To Varkala

Travel approximately 107 km to Varkala, check in and use the afternoon for the cliff, beach, cafes and sunset. Janardanaswamy Temple is an optional addition.

  • Coastal road transfer
  • Varkala Cliff and beach
  • Sunset and overnight stay
Day10

Varkala To Thiruvananthapuram

Travel to Thiruvananthapuram for Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple when current rules and timings allow. Choose only one further palace, museum, heritage or Kovalam option and stay overnight in the city.

  • Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple
  • Kuthiramalika Palace alternative
  • Thiruvananthapuram overnight

Visitor note: Temple entry is restricted to Hindus and strict traditional dress requirements apply. Verify current rules and timings. Visitors who cannot or prefer not to enter can use the same window for Kuthiramalika Palace or Napier Museum.

Day11

Thiruvananthapuram Departure

Keep sightseeing minimal and continue to the airport or railway station according to the confirmed departure time. The transport tools remain origin-aware rather than assuming one home city.

  • Relaxed breakfast
  • Optional short city stop
  • Airport or railway departure
